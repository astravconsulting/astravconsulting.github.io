Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This page explains what information Astrav Consulting collects through this website and how it is used.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you provide, which may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Area of interest and any message you include

This information is submitted through Web3Forms, a third-party form processing service, and forwarded to our team by email. We do not store form submissions in a separate database of our own.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use this site (e.g. which pages are viewed, general traffic patterns). Google Analytics may set cookies in your browser as part of this. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using tools such as the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you about our services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained in our email records for as long as reasonably needed to support our business relationship with you, or until you request deletion.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at info@astravconsulting.com.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to info@astravconsulting.com or through our contact page.
